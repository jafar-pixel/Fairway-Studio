## Asset provenance

- Original supplied export: `studio-editorial.png` and `moodboard-detail.png`. These remain preserved; they are different imagery from the mockup product shots.
- Recreated assets: standalone artwork generated using the supplied mockups as references, with prompts and hashes in `asset-manifest.md`. These are exploratory business starter assets, not original photo masters, approved brand marks, or flattened app screens.
- Eight additional user-supplied PNG originals were inspected and mapped: Slim silhouette golf bag; Three generations, one collection; Complete the look; Founders five; Detail challenge; Keep, change, remove; Generations on the fairway; Ageless golf style. All are full portrait concept posters/moodboards, stored privately byte-for-byte. None is the exact embedded mockup photo or an editable CF logo master. Detailed hidden typography/guideline tab contents, real Pin URLs and provider credentials were not supplied.

## Persistence boundaries

The reviewed and applied import targets the user's existing Fairway workspace, retains all existing records, deduplicates starter rows, and writes explicit origin metadata. It does not create eleven duplicate workspaces. Existing concepts become editable through their canonical idea/project/task/library records and reusable through saved relationships. Immutable versions and approvals retain their original evidence rules.
